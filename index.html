Not everything here walks. Crawlers come up walls and across ceilings, haul themselves along on tentacles, and leave a trail you can follow back. Shoot them and they come apart.

Reserve operatives stand frozen along the route. Walk into one to bring it up — it fights with you and draws fire. The same AI can fly the whole run if you would rather watch.

Crates and rubble are real weight — shoot them and they move, and they will stop a bullet meant for you. Some of the garrison has been got at: corrupted mercs hover, vent, and take more killing. Something much larger is already waiting near the extraction pad, and it throws the scenery.

Fight east to the extraction pad. Nothing here was drawn by hand — the walls, decks, skyline, weather and every merc in the level are generated from your build seed the moment you deploy.
